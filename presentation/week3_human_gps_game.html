WEEK 3 · GAME
Human GPS: Find the Places!
1
Work in pairs. One is the Walker. One is the Navigator.
2
Walker: close your eyes and ask, “Excuse me, how do I get to the ___?”
3
Navigator: guide your partner. Use: Go straight. Turn left. Turn right. Stop!
4
Navigator also: write the instructions in your notebook as you give them.
5
Switch roles for each place. At the end, we will check your notebooks!
CHALLENGE: You can’t touch each other to give instructions!
Only your voice and commands. Walk slowly.

Women’s restroom
Men’s restroom
The office
